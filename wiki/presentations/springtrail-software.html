ROM

08 · Original software · 04

Fixed point makes movement deterministic

The baseline rules use sixteenth-pixel units. A jump impulse of −5.25 pixels/frame is −84 units; gravity adds 4 units on that same update.

Impulse

−5.25 × 16 = −84

Gravity

0.25 × 16 = +4

First update

−84 + 4 = −80, or −5 pixels/frame before collision.

Explore the reasoning

Negative vertical motion goes upward in this coordinate system. These deterministic integer rules describe the current baseline; staged alignment can replace them only through its explicit contract and evidence.

08 · Original software · 05

World and camera are different spaces

The baseline camera maps world x into screen x. Collision remains in world coordinates, so scrolling cannot move a wall relative to the player.

Conceptual window: screen x = world x − camera x. Diagram is not to scale.

Explore the reasoning

At world x=100 with camera x=28, screen x=72. This arithmetic illustrates the anchor; camera clamping and collision stay in the canonical world-space rules.
