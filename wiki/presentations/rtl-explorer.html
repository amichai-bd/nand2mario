Every module, inside the one that builds it

This page draws the composed DE10-Lite design as nested blocks. One block is one module instance — plus, inside each parent, one tile for that parent's own logic. A block inside another block is instantiated there. Select any block to read what that module owns, its ports, and its real source.

Measured

Lines per file, register-macro invocations, ports with direction and width, and the instance tree — all read from the SystemVerilog by tools/wiki/rtl_modules.py. A generate for with a literal bound is expanded, so a three-bank store is three blocks.

Sets a block's size

Its area follows lines + 4 × register macros, and nothing else. The coefficient is a drawing choice; the two counts are measurements.

Not here

No cell, ALM or fitted-register figure. The repository keeps no fit report, and a whole-design fit cannot be divided back into modules. Rather than estimate one, this page shows none.

src/rtl/ holds 89 tracked SystemVerilog files and 11,294 lines: 75 modules (10,196 lines, 433 register macros after replication, 1,549 ports) and 14 packages (1,098 lines). n2m_v05_system instantiates 16 owners; several are themselves structural, so the shape below is a tree rather than a row.

5 generate for loops in these sources replicate an instance or a register macro, and every one of their bounds is a literal, so every count here is exact. Where a bound is not a literal the generator does not guess: that site counts once, the figure is marked a floor with a ≥, and the panel says so.

Explore the reasoning

Searching for always_ff would report zero registers for most of these modules. Every flop here is declared through a macro in src/rtl/common/macros.svh, so the macro invocations are the measurement. They are counted in the synthesis view, with SYNTHESIS defined, so a simulation-only branch is reported separately instead of being counted as hardware. A register macro is a declaration site, not a flop count: its Q may be a vector, so a module with 21 macros can hold more than 21 flops. The page counts and names macros, never flops.

How to read a block

A parent block holds its children plus one own logic tile. That tile is the parent's own lines and register macros — the wiring and arbitration it does itself — so a structural module reads as a thin frame around its children instead of an empty box.

A panel's summary is the comment the source carries above that module, or at the head of its file. Where that comment records provenance rather than behaviour, that is what the source says; the linked contract carries the behaviour. 9 modules carry no comment at all, and their panels say so instead of inventing one.

Areas are exact within one parent. Each parent also spends a label band and a small inset on its frame, so a deeply nested block is drawn a little smaller than a block of the same weight near the top. Compare siblings confidently; compare across depths loosely. L is the module's line count and R its register-macro count after generate replication; a ≥ before it marks a floor. A name that will not fit its block loses its n2m_ prefix, and then the block carries no label at all; its panel and its hover text always carry the full name.

Explore the reasoning

A worked size: n2m_ppu_timing is 200 lines with 21 register macros, so its weight is 284. n2m_cpu_execute is 412 lines with no register macros, so its weight is 412. The longer file draws larger even though it holds no state, which is the honest reading of the two measurements: one is a wide combinational decode, the other a dense sequencer.

What this page does not claim

Two boards are supported. The DE10-Lite (MAX 10 10M50DAF484C7G) is the qualified board: the tree above is rooted at its composed top and its bring-up is recorded. DE10-Nano (Cyclone V SE 5CSEBA6U23I7) support is in progress; no DE10-Nano top exists in the tree yet, so none is drawn here.

Generated, not written

tools/wiki/rtl_modules.py measures the sources and tools/wiki/rtl_explorer.py draws this page. A host test rebuilds it and fails when the committed page differs, so the diagram cannot drift away from the RTL.

Counted in the synthesis view

Registers and instances are read with SYNTHESIS defined. Simulation-only registers and instances are named in the panels rather than counted as hardware.

Still open

A second board needs its own device targets and its own clocking evidence: issue 809 and issue 810.

Explore the reasoning

A per-module cell count would answer a different question than this page does, and answering it would mean synthesising each module alone — a figure that would not match what the whole design fits to. Line and register counts are cheap, exact, and recomputed on every check. Where they mislead, the panel says so: a wide combinational decode is long and holds nothing.
