A pixel crosses three responsibilities

The PPU produces a two-bit final shade. The frame bridge stores complete images. VGA scanout maps that shade into monitor RGB.

Explore the reasoning

A final shade has already passed through the Game Boy rendering and palette rules. The VGA adapter does not rerun tile or sprite priority.

04 · Graphics · 02

Tile bytes become a shade

In the integration witness, tile rows use bytes 55 and 33 with identity palette E4. The checked image repeats shades 0, 1, 2, 3.

Leftmost four pixels: shade = 2 × high bit + low bit. The next four repeat.

Explore the reasoning

For the leftmost four bits, the low plane is 0,1,0,1 and the high plane is 0,0,1,1. Combining high×2 + low explains the stripe pattern without taking expected pixels from DUT output.

A complete frame has 23,040 pixels

The source accepts row-major shades with a start on the first pixel. Gaps are allowed; completion accompanies pixel index 23039.

Explore the reasoning

A partial image must not become a complete frame because a timeout expired. The passive observer sees completed source images even when presentation chooses to discard one.

04 · Graphics · 05

A bank swap prevents tearing

Scanout must not change its display bank partway through active output. The mailbox transfers ownership of complete frames at permitted boundaries.

Explore the reasoning

The producer and monitor run at slightly different rates. Holding or discarding complete images is compatible with coherence; showing half of two frames is not.

04 · Graphics · 06

Source proof and display proof differ

A correct source CRC does not prove RGB scanout. The source-to-VGA proof checks RGB replicas and corresponding canonical CRCs; the physical monitor gate remains open.

Source

Check every expected source shade and its identity.

Adapter

Check scale replicas, RGB and matching image identity.

Physical

Check the real monitor, pins and tolerances separately.

Explore the reasoning

A CRC is a compact image identity, not a substitute for a trustworthy expected image. Follow the gap register for the delivered component proof and its explicit physical limits.

Source: GAP-012 source-to-VGA evidence
