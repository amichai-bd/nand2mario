# Project stories

Dated engineering retrospectives describe what happened and what the evidence
supported at the time. They may cite completed issues and pull requests. They
are editorial history, not current behavior contracts or release certificates;
follow the linked owning specifications for current requirements.

- **14 September 2026:** [Building a Game Boy with AI agents](2026-09-13-remote-fpga.md) — a retrospective on the whole project, domain by domain: synthesized SystemVerilog on a MAX 10 rather than an emulator; running that board over one serial port from a phone; directing crewmates through the delivery loop, with three specification mistakes of mine and four of the agents' own from the pull-request record; the RTL and its exact timebase; the SM83 toolchain and two original games; the two axes of test independence and two homebrew games that never enable the LCD; the build, test-catalogue and publishing tooling; what the project does not do and what it cost; and the three moments that needed a person at the board.
