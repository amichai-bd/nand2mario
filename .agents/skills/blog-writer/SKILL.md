# Blog writer

Write for a technically curious reader who does not know the repository. Find
the engineering question and the concrete turning point before choosing a title.
Use the owner's account for personal experience; use dated repository records
for implementation and measured results. Do not manufacture scenes, quotations,
feelings, chronology or causal claims to make the narrative smoother.

Keep a compact claim ledger while drafting: observation, owner recollection,
inference or unresolved limit, with the source that supports each material claim.
The ledger is a working artifact, not another published specification. Link the
most useful primary evidence close to the claim. Explain the constraint, the
choice, what failed and what the evidence changed. A passing component test does
not establish whole-system compatibility; a framebuffer capture is not a camera
view of the board's monitor.

Use dated Markdown under wiki/blogs and link its index. Historical issue/PR
citations belong there; normative behavior remains at its owning contract.
Personal prices, model labels and rough day counts should retain their attribution
and uncertainty rather than being converted into current provider facts. Keep
private infrastructure, credentials and undisclosed personal details out.

Choose length and structure for the requested reader. Favor concrete mechanisms,
measured units and instructive failures over promotional claims. Make a substantial
summary stand on its own when requested; let the main narrative develop the
tradeoffs instead of repeating it. Reuse authorized existing images with accurate
captions and source links. Do not generate illustrative evidence.

Consult [research notes](references/technical-writing.md) when writing for a Hacker
News-style technical audience. Writing the article does not authorize submitting
it or posting comments. Verify current platform rules before a separately
requested external action. Have an independent reader check the main factual
claims, narrative clarity and limits; run the repository's document/link/browser
checks and validate this skill after material edits.
