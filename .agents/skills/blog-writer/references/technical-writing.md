# Writing for a technical reader

Research checked on 2026-09-13. These sources inform editorial choices; none
predicts votes, traffic or acceptance.

- [Hacker News guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html):
  curiosity, factual titles and original sources matter; avoid promotion and
  vote solicitation. The prohibition on generated or AI-edited text applies to
  comments. An off-site AI-assisted draft is not an HN comment. This skill does
  not authorize submitting either one.
- [Show HN rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html): an ordinary blog
  post is a regular submission. Hardware can require a video or detailed article
  to make the work accessible, but an article is not automatically a Show HN.
- [Paul Graham, Write Like You Talk](https://www.paulgraham.com/writing44.html):
  conversational revision helps expose unnecessarily formal wording. Read a
  difficult paragraph aloud and rewrite it without losing the mechanism.
- [Julia Evans, Some blogging myths](https://jvns.ca/blog/2023/06/05/some-blogging-myths/):
  specific experience can be useful without teaching every prerequisite or
  appealing to everyone. Name the reader and the problem they can recognize.
- [Julia Evans, Blog about what you've struggled with](https://jvns.ca/blog/2021/05/24/blog-about-what-you-ve-struggled-with/):
  explain what finally resolved the confusion. A failure becomes useful when
  the reader can see the missing distinction or the check that exposed it.
- [Eli Lipsitz, Introducing GameBub](https://eli.lipsitz.net/posts/introducing-gamebub/):
  a relevant hardware example combines a demonstration, architecture, component
  tradeoffs, failures and source access. Its
  [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43027335) is evidence of
  discussion, not proof that a particular writing technique caused reception.

Editorial synthesis: show the artifact early, then explain a few consequential
choices with measurements and failures. Give the reader enough architecture to
understand the constraint. Link reproduction details instead of copying a manual.
Separate measured behavior from intended scope. A factual title and readable
headings help readers decide whether the article answers their question; neither
is a substitute for an interesting, honest account.
